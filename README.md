# Healthcare Appointment No-Show Reduction

## Project Overview

A Healthcare Business Analyst project focused on reducing patient appointment no-shows and improving appointment slot utilization.

## Business Problem

Patients may miss scheduled appointments due to forgotten appointments, scheduling conflicts, transportation issues, or lack of timely reminders.

## Project Objective

To design a solution that helps the hospital:

* Reduce appointment no-shows
* Improve appointment confirmation
* Enable easy rescheduling
* Track cancellation reasons
* Improve appointment utilization
* Provide management reporting
